# Panelavo 0.1.80

## Changed

- Added optional 80-character website labels during creation and in Settings.
- Bound labels to both the normalized domain and current upstream site-record ID, and remove them when a site is deleted.
- Simplified the website list to Domain, Label, Type, and Actions, with mobile cards showing label, domain, and type.
- Grouped websites by the canonical Project-category order, placed legacy/external sites under Uncategorized, and kept linked services beneath their parent.
- Expanded search to labels, domains and aliases, types, categories, applications, and linked-service names.
- Moved runtime, site user, status, and creation date into a website-specific details card in Settings.

Existing installations need no migration. Old or mismatched label records are ignored safely.
